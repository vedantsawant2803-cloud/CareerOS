# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CareerOS a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experience
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Taking responsibility and apologizing for mistakes

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without permission
- Sharing private contact details (phone numbers, personal emails) of others
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Impersonation

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer at
**vedantsawant2803@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to maintain
confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project
leadership.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is
representing the project.
